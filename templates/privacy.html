Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information only to run the expense tracker: to sign you in, show your spending summaries and filter your records by date. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on the servers that run Spendly. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorised access, but no system is completely secure. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated records at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic request information such as your IP address. We do not otherwise share your personal data with third-party services except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your data rights, contact the Spendly team.

This policy may change from time to time. Changes take effect when the "Last updated" date is revised.
